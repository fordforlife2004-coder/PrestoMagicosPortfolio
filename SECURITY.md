## Reporting a Vulnerability

Too report a vulnerability please email fordforlife2004@gmail.com

Vulnerabilities that are reported will be updated right away! When reporting please explain the type of vulnerability, the danger of it, what it may or could cause. What is harmful about it. 
Any reports not matching the given criteria will not be taken into consideration and thrown and burned (Metaphorically).

## Reporting a Bug

When reporting a bug please explain what the bug was, What it did, and what the process should be doing! Report too the same email for vulnerabilities.
